Privacy Policy

Loveira

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Loveira is an AI-powered companion app designed for text-based conversations. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Loveira.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use Loveira, the app may process information you provide in conversations, companion preferences, selected personality or chat modes, memories you choose to create or that are created to support conversation continuity, app settings, and purchase or subscription status.

Loveira may also process limited technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as a device or installation identifier, app version, operating system information, diagnostic information, and service usage information.

2. AI Conversations

Messages you send may be processed by AI systems in order to generate responses and provide the companion experience. Do not share passwords, financial account details, government identification numbers, or other information you do not want processed as part of a conversation.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide chat responses, maintain conversation context and memories, personalize companion behavior, manage message allowances and premium access, troubleshoot problems, protect the service from abuse, and improve reliability and user experience.

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Loveira does not receive your full payment card information. We may receive transaction and entitlement information necessary to unlock purchases, restore purchases, and manage premium access.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate Loveira, such as AI processing, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or other infrastructure providers used by the app. Information is not sold for monetary consideration. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to technical, legal, fraud-prevention, and security requirements. Data stored only on your device may be removed when you delete the app or clear that data, depending on the feature.

7. Your Choices

Where supported by the app, you can manage or delete conversation history, memories, and preferences. You may also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with you, subject to applicable law and information we are required to retain.

8. Children

Loveira is not intended for children. Users must meet the minimum age shown for Loveira in the App Store and any additional age requirement applicable in their country or region.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for such processing.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as Loveira changes. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
