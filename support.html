Purchases & Message Packs

If a purchase completed but your message balance did not update, restart Loveira and check your connection. If the issue remains, contact support with the product purchased and approximate purchase time. Do not send full payment-card details.

Subscriptions

Loveira+ subscriptions purchased through the App Store are managed through your Apple account. You can view or cancel an active subscription from your Apple subscription settings. If premium access is missing after reinstalling Loveira, use Restore Purchases in the app where available.

AI Conversations

Loveira uses artificial intelligence, so responses can sometimes be inaccurate or unexpected. Loveira is a virtual AI companion, not a real person or professional adviser.
